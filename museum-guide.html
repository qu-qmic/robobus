User Experience

Bob's Journey

From booking in London to a farewell at MIA Park — follow Bob's seamless layover adventure

1

Booking the Tour

Bob, a London-based traveller and Qatar Airways regular, is planning his trip to Hong Kong. Recognising his 8-hour transfer in Doha, Qatar Airways presents a personalised museum tour offer during the layover. A lover of Qatar's cultural attractions, Bob readily accepts while finalising his booking from the comfort of home.

2

Configuring the Guide

En route to Doha aboard the Qatar Airways cabin, Bob uses the inflight entertainment system to configure his upcoming tour. He selects the Museum of Islamic Art (MIA) in the Al Corniche area via the large seatback screen, finalises a schedule that fits within his transit time, and activates the context-aware notification service

3

Welcoming Bob at HIA

Upon landing at Hamad International Airport (HIA), Bob is met with personalised service the moment he steps out of the terminal. A sleek white-and-maroon Karwa autonomous vehicle waits with a prominent digital sign welcoming him by his chosen nickname — "BOBBY-Q"

4

Transfer from HIA to the Museum

Relaxing inside the Karwa autonomous vehicle, Bob enjoys the smooth ride along the Doha Corniche towards the museum. An integrated display screen surfaces personalised information about passing attractions and upcoming events, curated to match the preferences he configured on his flight

5

Arriving at the Islamic Museum

The Karwa vehicle arrives at the stunning geometric Museum of Islamic Art. Bob steps out and walks directly to the main entrance gate, entering seamlessly by scanning the QR-code ticket generated and sent to his mobile phone during the inflight configuration — no queues, no delays

6

Receiving a Nearby Friend Notification

While immersed in the Textiles gallery, Bob's phone vibrates. The context-aware service he activated has detected a familiar connection nearby: Alice, a friend from a previous Qatar Museum visit. The notification displays Alice's picture and her current location in the adjacent MIA Park

7

Messaging Alice

Bob immediately messages Alice via the app, proposing a meeting in MIA Park in one hour. Alice agrees. Bob accepts the suggested itinerary update — the tour plan adjusts automatically and a dedicated Museum Autonomous Mobile Pod is dispatched to facilitate his short transfer to Alice's location in the park

8

Driving to Alice's Location in MIA Park

Having concluded his museum visit, Bob exits the building to find the allocated Museum Autonomous Mobile Pod waiting. The smaller, agile, transparent bubble vehicle navigates park pathways with a short, scenic drive through the greenery toward Alice's location by the waterfront

9

Ordering Refreshments & Purchasing Souvenirs

While the transparent Pod winds through the park, Bob takes advantage of onboard services. He orders a Dino brand ice cream from the internal display, while a curated selection of digital souvenirs appears alongside a Qatar Airways offer for an extra 7 kg baggage allowance. Bob accepts the offer and finalises his purchases seamlessly inside the moving pod

10

Meeting Alice with Refreshments

The Pod arrives at Alice's location by the water's edge in MIA Park, where a cheerful Coffee Robobus serves coffees and ice creams. They enjoy their refreshments together at a nearby table. Moments later, a small autonomous Museum Delivery drone arrives, dropping off the souvenirs Bob purchased just minutes earlier inside the pod

11

Driving Back to the Airport

Bob bids Alice farewell. Right on schedule, a Karwa Autonomous vehicle picks him up from the MIA Park entrance. As the vehicle merges onto the highway toward Hamad International Airport, the large interior display generates a personalised tour summary — key pictures and an AI-generated short video clip, letting him relive the entire layover experience

Technology Enablers

Advanced Systems Integration

Cutting-edge technologies working in concert to deliver a flawless, context-aware museum experience

Context-Aware AI Engine

Real-time analysis of visitor location, behaviour, social connections, and schedule to dynamically update itineraries and surface relevant notifications

Autonomous Vehicle Fleet

Karwa autonomous vehicles and Museum Mobile Pods equipped with LiDAR, cameras, and radar for safe, geo-fenced navigation through urban and park environments

Inflight Configuration System

Integrated seatback entertainment API allowing passengers to plan, configure, and activate their entire layover tour while still aboard the Qatar Airways flight

Instant ticket generation and delivery to mobile wallets enabling contactless, queue-free entry at museum gates and cultural attractions

Last-mile souvenir and refreshment delivery via museum-operated autonomous drones, guided by GPS to the visitor's precise park location

AI Content Generation

On-trip photo capture and post-visit AI video synthesis create a personalised highlight reel delivered to the passenger before their return flight boards
