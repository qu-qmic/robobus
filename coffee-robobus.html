User Experience

The Journey

From discovery to enjoyment, every step is seamless and delightful

1

Discover & Approach

Spot the Coffee Robobus through digital signage, social media, or while strolling through luxury districts. Approach the vehicle at designated stops or when it parks nearby.

2

Browse Digital Menu

View available coffee drinks, Gelati Dino ice cream flavors, and current promotions on digital displays with detailed descriptions and pricing.

3

Customize & Order

Place your order via touchscreen or QR code, customizing coffee (size, milk type, sweetness, add-ons) or selecting your favorite Gelati Dino gelato flavors and combinations.

4

Contactless Payment

Complete secure payment through integrated POS system with support for all major payment methods.

5

Watch Preparation

Observe as the robotic barista expertly crafts your coffee or as premium Gelati Dino gelato is carefully served, ensuring perfect quality every time.

6

Choose Your Experience

Sit inside enjoying the premium ambiance and comfortable seating, or take your order to enjoy outside nearby.
