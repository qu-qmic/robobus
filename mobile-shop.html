Student Experience

From Notification to Pickup in Minutes

Seamless retail integrated into campus life

1

Spot & Track the Robobus

Students receive push notifications when the robobus arrives near the library, student center, or engineering building. Real-time map shows ETA.

2

Enter & Browse

Step into the climate-controlled robobus. Explore clearly labeled sections or scan QR codes to view the full catalog on your phone.

3

Order & Contactless Pay

Use the onboard touchscreen or mobile app to select items. Tap student ID or credit card for instant payment — digital receipt sent via app.

4

Pickup & Go

Collect bagged items at the counter, exit the robobus, and continue to class — saving up to 20 minutes compared to walking to a fixed store.
