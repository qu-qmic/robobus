Opportunity & Market Motivation

The Dual Infrastructure Challenge

Rapid growth in connected vehicles, smart cities, and AI-driven services creates unprecedented demand for flexible infrastructure.

Edge Computing Demand

Data-intensive applications require low-latency processing that fixed infrastructure can't always provide

EV Charging Pressure

Electric vehicle adoption strains charging infrastructure in high-demand and underserved areas

Static Infrastructure Limits

Fixed installations cannot respond dynamically to fluctuating compute and energy demands

Peak Demand Challenges

Events and emergencies create temporary spikes that permanent infrastructure struggles to handle

Underserved Locations

Remote or temporary sites lack access to both computational resources and energy supply

Capital Investment Barriers

High costs of deploying extensive fixed infrastructure across all potential demand locations

Service Scenarios

Real-World Applications

Flexible deployment across diverse scenarios where compute and energy resources are needed

On-Demand EV Charging

EV drivers request charging via mobile app. The mobile unit navigates to their location and provides emergency or supplemental charging service.

Smart City Applications

Deployed to high-traffic areas to support real-time AI applications such as traffic analytics, video processing, and urban monitoring.

Large Events Support

Provides temporary edge computing capacity for AR/VR services, crowd monitoring, media streaming, and enhanced visitor experiences.

Emergency Response

Supplies backup power to critical infrastructure or devices during emergencies and disaster situations.
